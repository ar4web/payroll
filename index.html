        Deploy & Offline

        1. Local: double-click index.html — works from file://, no server, no install.
        2. GitHub Pages: push index.html + README.md to a repo → Settings → Pages → Deploy from branch (root).
        3. Offline: SheetJS & Chart.js load from CDN and are cached by your browser; if unavailable, the app falls back to built-in charts and CSV export.
        4. Your master, timesheets, mappings, highlights and templates persist in localStorage on this device.
